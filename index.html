HOW TO PLAY

📋 OVERVIEW

You are a prisoner trapped in a deadly complex of 25 rooms, broadcast on a lethal reality TV show. Your goal: find Room 25 — the exit — and escape before time runs out!

The complex is a 5×5 grid of rooms. Most rooms are face-down (unknown). Some are safe, some are deadly traps. You must explore, cooperate, and survive.

🎮 GAME PHASES

Each round has two phases:

  1. PROGRAMMING: Each player secretly selects 2 actions.
  2. RESOLUTION: Players take turns resolving their actions one at a time.

⚡ ACTIONS

👁
LOOK

Secretly peek at an adjacent face-down room. Only you see what's there!

🏃
MOVE

Move your character to an adjacent room (up/down/left/right). The room is revealed and its effect triggers.

👊
PUSH

Push another player in your room to an adjacent room. That room is revealed and its effect triggers on the pushed player.

⚙
CONTROL

Slide an entire row or column of rooms in any direction. Rooms and players shift together. The room that slides off reappears on the other side.

🏠 ROOM TYPES

🟢 SAFE ROOMS
  • Empty Room: Nothing happens. Safe to be in.
  • Vision Chamber: Peek at any hidden room on the board.
  • Moving Chamber: Swap this room with any hidden room.
  • Control Chamber: Perform a free Control action.
🟡 OBSTACLE ROOMS
  • Vortex Room: Sends you back to the Central Room.
  • Freezer Room: You lose your next action.
  • Dark Room: Peek is disabled from this room.
🔴 DEADLY ROOMS
  • Mortal Chamber: Instant death!
  • Trapped Chamber: You must leave by your next action or die.
  • Acid Bath: If 2+ players are here, one dies.
  • Flooded Room: If you end 2 consecutive turns here, you drown.
🔵 SPECIAL
  • Central Room: Starting room. Safe zone, no pushing allowed.
  • Room 25: THE EXIT! Get all prisoners here, then use Control to slide it off the board to win!

🏆 WINNING

Cooperative: All surviving prisoners must be in Room 25, then one player uses Control to slide it off the grid. Everyone escapes together!

Suspicion: Guards try to prevent escape. Prisoners must find Room 25 AND figure out who the guards are.

Competition: Only YOU need to be in Room 25 when it slides off. Push others into traps!

💀 LOSING

If the countdown reaches zero before you escape, all prisoners die. If all prisoners are eliminated, the game ends immediately.
